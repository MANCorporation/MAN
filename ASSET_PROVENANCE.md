# Asset provenance status

The following icon collections are PNG exports of Haiku icon artwork:

- `ICONS/`
- `overlay/usr/share/icons/`
- `theme_/`

Source: the Haiku source tree artwork, exported as PNG through Icon-O-Matic;
the `darealshinji/haiku-icons` export repository identifies the Haiku source
commit and states that non-trademark graphics are MIT-licensed.

Status: **third-party MIT**. These assets are not covered by the MAN
proprietary license and must not be represented as MAN-owned. Preserve the
Haiku copyright and MIT notice. Do not use any Haiku trademark (including the
Haiku name, logo, or leaf marks) as MAN branding.

The separate rEFInd boot assets are documented in
`scripts/bootloader/THIRD_PARTY-ICON-LICENSES.md` and are covered by the
third-party notices instead.
