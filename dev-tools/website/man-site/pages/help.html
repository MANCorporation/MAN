Questions

The things people ask most often, in plain words.

Getting Started

  • {i:emote-love}Is MAN really free?{i:pan-down}

    Yes. MAN is free to download, free to use and free to update. There are no ads and no paid extras.

  • {i:computer}Will MAN work on my computer?{i:pan-down}

    Most Windows PCs and Intel Macs from the last ten years work. Macs with M1, M2 and M3 work too; Macs with M4 and newer not yet.

    The easiest way to know: put MAN on a USB stick and try it. Nothing on your computer changes until you choose to install.

  • {i:document-save}Will my files and Windows stay?{i:pan-down}

    With DUHACH!, yes: it makes room for MAN next to Windows and leaves your files and Windows as they are. Each time the computer starts, you choose MAN or Windows.

    Installing from a USB stick also offers to put MAN next to Windows. Only if you choose "erase" is the disk cleared, and MAN asks before it does that.

  • {i:preferences-desktop-locale}Which languages does MAN speak?{i:pan-down}

    MAN's welcome lets you choose from 74 languages, and the keyboard follows your country.

Using MAN

  • {i:software-update-available}How do updates work?{i:pan-down}

    MAN looks for updates once a day and tells you when one is ready. It is checked to be really from MAN, installed when you restart, and your files and apps stay.

    Computers without internet can update from a USB stick with the update file.

  • {i:edit-undo}What if something goes wrong?{i:pan-down}

    MAN remembers how your computer was set up, every day and before every update. Open Snapshots to go back to a day when everything worked. Your own files are not changed.

    If MAN does not start at all, choose an earlier day in the start menu.

  • {i:view-app-grid}How do I get apps?{i:pan-down}

    Open Software and click Install, or browse the apps page. Apps you download from a website (.deb, Flatpak, AppImage) open in App Installer with one click.

  • {i:application-x-executable}Can I use my Windows programs?{i:pan-down}

    Many of them. Open the program and MAN runs it, after checking it for harmful software. Some programs, especially games with anti-cheat, may not work.

  • {i:system-reboot}How do I go back to Windows or macOS?{i:pan-down}

    On a PC, choose Windows when the computer starts. On a Mac, choose "Restart to macOS" in MAN's menu at the top right, or pick what starts every time in Settings → Startup Disk.
