MAN 10 TEST 6

November 17, 2026 · View on GitHub

MAN now works on Macs with M1, M2 and M3. A new helper, DUHACH! for Mac, gets your Mac ready in a few clicks, and macOS keeps working exactly as before. Already using MAN 10? TEST 6 arrives by itself through Settings → Updates.

{i:folder-download} Get TEST 6
  • {stile:slate:computer}MAN on Macs with Apple's chipsMacBook Air, MacBook Pro, iMac, Mac mini and Mac Studio with M1, M2 and M3. Try it from a USB stick, then install it on an external drive or next to macOS.
  • {stile:blue:system-software-install}DUHACH! for MacChecks your Mac, asks whether you want only MAN's start-up files or also some space for MAN, and shows how much room is free. Then it does the work.
  • {stile:green:system-reboot}macOS one click away"Restart to macOS" in MAN's menu at the top right. In Settings → Startup Disk, choose what your Mac starts every time.
  • {stile:teal:drive-removable-media}Install on This MacShows only your external drives and the space DUHACH! made, so macOS is never touched.
  • {stile:orange:applications-system}Start menu fix for PCsOn some PCs with more than one disk, the start menu could stop working after an update. Fixed.
