MAN 10 TEST 7

November 19, 2026 · View on GitHub

A new start-up, MAN Recovery and a new look. MAN now starts straight away with its logo and a progress bar. If something goes wrong, MAN Recovery helps you fix it. Already using MAN 10? TEST 7 arrives by itself through Settings → Updates.

{i:folder-download} Get TEST 7
  • {stile:green:system-reboot}A new start-upThe MAN logo and a progress bar instead of text, from the moment you press the power button until the login screen.
  • {stile:blue:computer}Choose what startsHold Alt (Option on a Mac keyboard) while the computer starts: MAN, Windows, macOS, a USB stick or MAN Recovery appear as big icons. Ctrl+Enter makes your choice the one that starts every time.
  • {stile:orange:edit-undo}MAN RecoveryIf MAN does not start, choose MAN Recovery: go back to an earlier day, or reinstall MAN while keeping your files and accounts.
  • {stile:purple:dialog-password}A new login screenYour own picture behind the login and lock screens. Choose it in Settings.
  • A new logo and soundThe green MAN logo everywhere, and MAN's own sound when something needs your attention.
